# Music library licences

All `mixkit-*.mp3` files: Mixkit Stock Music Free License (https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree), checked 2026-10-10.
Allowed: social media video posts, online marketing ads, podcasts, YouTube; commercial + non-commercial; no attribution required.
Not allowed: CDs/DVDs, TV/radio broadcast, video games, remixing into a music-only track, claiming as own, registering on a rights-management (Content ID) service.
If a platform claim appears, forward details to team@mixkit.co.
Source URL pattern: https://assets.mixkit.co/music/<id>/<id>.mp3
