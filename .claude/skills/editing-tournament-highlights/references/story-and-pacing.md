# Story & Pacing

## The master arc: "From Arrival to Legacy"

Every tournament video tells the same emotional story: *ordinary people showed
up, fought for something, and became something together.* Map footage onto it.

| # | Beat | Job | Feeling | Typical footage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **HOOK** | Stop the scroll | Whoa | Single best action frame: strike, dive, smash, net shake. Often slow-mo. |
| 1 | **ARRIVAL** | Set the stage | Anticipation | Lights on, turf wide, laces, warm-ups, team huddle, walking in |
| 2 | **BATTLE** | Show it was hard | Intensity | Fast rally of tackles, sprints, saves, rallies, shouts. Quickest cutting. |
| 3 | **PEAK** | The defining moment | Release | The winning goal / match point → slow-mo → crowd erupts |
| 4 | **GLORY** | Crown the winner | Pride | Final score card, trophy, champions' names, MVP |
| 5 | **BOND** | Why it matters | Warmth | Handshakes, hugs, losers clapping, group photo, laughter |
| 6 | **TAG** | Brand, quietly | Belonging | Club logo + date + "Thrill Mill Club" (1.5s) |

**The silence rule:** the beat *just before* PEAK (last 0.3–0.6s of BATTLE)
drops the music low or cuts it to a riser/heartbeat. The peak hits with full sound.

## Beat sheets by length

**35s (Memory Engine default), ~18 shots**
| Beat | Time | Shots | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOOK | 0.0–2.0 | 1–2 | Slow-mo best moment, title slams at ~1.6s |
| ARRIVAL | 2.0–7.0 | 3 | Tournament name + date lower-third |
| BATTLE | 7.0–17.0 | 6–7 | Cut on beats, 1–1.5s each, team names flash |
| PEAK | 17.0–23.0 | 2–3 | Pre-peak silence, then slow-mo + speed ramp |
| GLORY | 23.0–29.0 | 2–3 | Final score card (≥2.5s), champion name |
| BOND | 29.0–33.5 | 2 | Group photo hold, warm grade |
| TAG | 33.5–35.0 | 1 | Logo, fade out on music tail |

**60s full recap:** same arc, BATTLE expands into 2–3 mini-chapters (one per
key match: "Quarter-final", "Semi-final"), each with its own score card.
Add a mid-video "breather" (2–3s of a slower shot/laugh) at ~30s.

**15s teaser (social cut):** HOOK 0–2 · BATTLE 2–8 · PEAK 8–11 · GLORY 11–14 · TAG 14–15.

## Shot-scoring rubric (score every candidate during triage)

Score 0–2 on each; keep ≥6 of 10. Hook/peak candidates need ≥8.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Action/Emotion** | Static, nothing happens | Some movement | Decisive action or visible emotion (shout, hug, celebration) |
| **Clarity** | Blurry/shaky/backlit | Usable | Sharp, well lit, subject clear |
| **Composition** | Subject tiny or cut off | OK | Subject fills frame, readable in 9:16 crop |
| **Story value** | Generic | Supports a beat | *Is* a beat (the winning goal, the trophy) |
| **Uniqueness** | Duplicate of a better shot | Similar to others | Only shot of its kind |

Tie-breakers: faces over backs; the winning team over others *except in BOND*,
where you show both sides; vary the people on screen; and include every team at least once
in a tournament recap (everyone who played should see themselves).

## Finding moments without watching

- **Loudness peaks** from triage = cheers, whistles, goals. Check 1–3s *before*
  the peak; that's usually the action, and the peak itself is the reaction.
- **Scene cuts** inside one clip mean the camera operator stopped and restarted, or
  panned hard. Treat each segment as its own shot.
- **Contact sheet patterns:** players converging/running toward one point = celebration;
  ball near net/goal = attempt; people still and standing = break (skip
  unless it's a huddle or group photo).
- Before committing a hook/peak, extract 0.2s-step frames around it and confirm
  the action actually happens on screen.

## Writing on-screen text & narration

- **Voice:** warm, proud, cinematic, never cheesy or corporate. Write like a commentator who
  knows these people. Short sentences. Present tense.
- **Text cards:** ≤5 words each. Examples by beat:
  HOOK: *"{Tournament name}"* · ARRIVAL: *"{Date} · {Venue}"* ·
  BATTLE: *"{n} teams. One crown."* · PEAK: *"Match point."* ·
  GLORY: *"Champions — {Team}"* + score · BOND: *"Same turf. Same family."* ·
  TAG: *"Thrill Mill Club"*.
- **Narration (optional):** 55–75 words for 35s (≈2.2 words/s, leaving room for
  music-only peaks). There's no narration over the PEAK; let the moment play.
  Example cadence: *"Saturday night. Eight teams. One turf. … Every pass
  mattered. Every sprint counted. … And then — this. … {Team}. Champions.
  But tonight, everyone who stepped on this turf wrote the story."*
- **Facts in text are sacred:** team names, scores, dates come only from the
  facts sheet. The script never paraphrases a number.

## Pacing checks before render

- Average shot length in BATTLE < 1.5s; in BOND > 2.0s (the video should slow down as it gets warmer).
- No two consecutive shots of the same framing (wide→wide) unless it's a deliberate rhythm.
- No text card on screen while another is still animating out.
- The longest hold in the video is either the score card or the final group shot.
