# Style Presets

Four complete looks. Pick one per video; offer a second as the alternative.
All fonts are Google Fonts (SIL OFL), free to use commercially. Thrill Mill brand
palette (from the app): navy `#0B2545` / `#0B1F3F`, teal `#0E7C86`, orange
`#EA5F14`, amber `#F5A524`. Every preset can swap its accent for brand orange or teal.

---

## 1. Luxury Noir: "black-tie championship"

*Elegant, premium, cherished. For champions' memory videos and manager pitches.*

- **Type:** display **Cormorant Garamond 600/700 italic** or **Playfair Display 800**;
  support **Montserrat 500**, letter-spaced +12–20%, uppercase, small.
  Numerals: Playfair Display (lining).
- **Palette:** near-black `#0A0A0B`, ivory `#F4EFE6`, champagne gold `#C9A45C`
  (gradient `#E6CC8A → #A67C33` for titles), navy `#0B1F3F` for cards.
- **Grade:** lifted blacks slightly, contrast +, saturation −15%, warm highlights,
  teal-less shadows; 15% vignette. BOND section warmer.
- **Motion:** slow, confident. Entries 0.6s `power3.out`, mask reveals, letter-spacing
  collapse, thin gold rules drawing in (line draw 0.5s). Gentle Ken Burns on stills (1.00→1.06).
- **Signature transition:** luma/gradient wipe or mask reveal through type. Use it 3×.
- **Music:** cinematic piano/strings + soft pulse, 75–95 BPM, big swell at the peak.
- **Frame details:** thin gold frame line 24px in from the edges on title cards; small
  caps "EST. THRILL MILL CLUB" tag.

## 2. Stadium Energy: "Nike-ad hype"

*Bold, loud, fast. For the social teaser and younger teams.*

- **Type:** display **Anton** or **Bebas Neue**, huge (200–320px), tight tracking,
  uppercase; support **Archivo 700** or **Inter 700**. Outline text (stroke only) layered behind solid text.
- **Palette:** black `#000`, white, brand orange `#EA5F14` as the single hot accent,
  electric teal `#0E7C86` secondary.
- **Grade:** high contrast, crushed blacks, saturation +10% on kits, cool shadows;
  slight sharpen.
- **Motion:** snappy. Entries 0.25–0.35s `expo.out`, kinetic words on beats,
  shake on impact (2–4px, 6 frames), zoom punch-ins on beats, speed ramps on every big action.
- **Signature transition:** whip pan + zoom punch-through. Up to 4×, plus 2 flash frames max.
- **Music:** 130–150 BPM sports anthem with a hard drop; impact SFX on title slams.
- **Frame details:** a big ghost number or team initial behind the subject; stripe/slash graphics in orange.

## 3. Golden Hour Film: "the memory reel"

*Nostalgic, warm, wholesome. For the "cherish this forever" family/community feel.*

- **Type:** display **DM Serif Display** or **Fraunces 600 (soft)**; support
  **Inter 400/500**, sentence case. Handwritten accent (sparingly): **Caveat** for dates/notes.
- **Palette:** cream `#F6EFE2`, warm brown `#3B2A1E`, sunset amber `#F5A524`, muted teal `#5E9C9A`.
- **Grade:** warm (+WB), soft contrast, faded blacks, gentle halation; 35mm grain
  (8–12%), subtle gate weave optional. Light leaks.
- **Motion:** organic and gentle. Fades + soft blur entries (0.5–0.7s), polaroid
  stills dropping in with a slight rotation (±3°), slow push-ins.
- **Signature transition:** light leak/film burn. 2–3×, plus dip-to-white once before BOND.
- **Music:** acoustic/indie build, 90–110 BPM, hand claps, uplifting.
- **Frame details:** rounded-corner polaroid frames for the group photo; date stamp
  in the corner like an old camcorder ("SAT · 12 OCT").

## 4. Club Heritage: "the official club film"

*Brand-forward, trustworthy, crisp. For Thrill Mill's own channels and app in-feed.*

- **Type:** display **Montserrat 800** (or **Sora 700**) uppercase; support
  **Inter 500**. Clean and geometric.
- **Palette:** brand navy `#0B2545` cards, teal `#0E7C86` interactive accents, orange
  `#EA5F14` single highlight (winner/score), white text.
- **Grade:** clean natural, balanced contrast, true skin tones, slight clarity.
- **Motion:** precise. Slide + fade 0.4s `power2.out`, scoreboard-style flip
  digits, lower-thirds with a teal bar wipe.
- **Signature transition:** navy panel wipe carrying the logo mark. 2–3×.
- **Music:** modern upbeat electronic/pop instrumental, 110–125 BPM.
- **Frame details:** consistent lower-third template (team name + match label),
  logo bug top-right at 70% opacity.

## Choosing

| Club/brief says | Lead with | Offer as alt |
|---|---|---|
| "premium", "elegant", "luxury", "cherish" | Luxury Noir | Golden Hour Film |
| "hype", "energy", "Instagram", "reels" | Stadium Energy | Club Heritage |
| "memories", "family", "community", kids/veterans event | Golden Hour Film | Luxury Noir |
| "official", "promote the club", app feed | Club Heritage | Stadium Energy |

Mixing rule: you may borrow ONE element across presets (e.g. Luxury Noir type with
Stadium Energy speed ramps). Never mix two type systems or two signature transitions.

When the user/manager reacts to a style, record the specifics (what they liked,
what they rejected) in `tournament-memory/style-log.md` and treat it as
higher priority than these defaults next time.
